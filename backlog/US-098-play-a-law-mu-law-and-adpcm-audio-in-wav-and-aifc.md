# US-098 — Play A-law, µ-law and ADPCM audio in WAV and AIFC

**Status:** 📋 Ready (filed 2026-11-07 from the media-format survey) · **Epic:** Media formats ·
**Blocked by:** US-097 (these files must first get past the detection it changes) · **Blocks:** none

## Story
As an application developer whose users bring telephony recordings and compressed voice or game assets,
I want A-law, µ-law and ADPCM audio in WAV and AIFC files to play,
so that these long-established encodings play like PCM.

## Problem
Paths starting `ji/` are under `modules/javafx.media/src/main/java/com/sun/media/jfxmediaimpl/`, `N/` under
`modules/javafx.media/src/main/native/`, `G/` under `N/gstreamer/gstreamer-lite/`, and `R/` under
`G/gst-plugins-base/gst-libs/gst/riff/`.
- **Refused before the parser.** With `fmt ` first, any format tag other than PCM or float throws "Compressed WAVE is
  not supported!" (`ji/MediaUtils.java:114-118`). US-097 keeps every encoding nothing decodes failing that early.
- **The parser knows them, nothing decodes them.** `riff-media.c` gives MS ADPCM (format tag 0x0002)
  `audio/x-adpcm` with layout `microsoft` (`R/riff-media.c:1305-1325`), A-law (0x0006) `audio/x-alaw`
  (`:1367-1386`), µ-law (0x0007) `audio/x-mulaw` (`:1404-1423`), and IMA ADPCM (0x0011) `audio/x-adpcm` with layout
  `dvi` (`:1428-1448`), and treats the A-law and µ-law sub-formats of `WAVE_FORMAT_EXTENSIBLE` the same way
  (`:1710-1752`). The tags are at `R/riff-ids.h:301-309`. gstreamer-lite registers no element that decodes these
  (`G/projects/plugins/gstplugins-lite.c:34-67`), and no `alawdec`, `mulawdec` or `adpcmdec` exists under `N/`
  (grep).
- **AIFC stops at its header.** aiffparse plays only the compression types `NONE`, `sowt`, `fl32`/`FL32` and `fl64`,
  and fails any other, A-law and µ-law included, with "Unsupported compression in AIFC"
  (`G/gst-plugins-bad/gst/aiff/aiffparse.c:735-760`, `:777-784`).
- **The specifications.** ITU-T G.711 defines A-law and µ-law by code tables
  (https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.711). IMA ADPCM is defined by the IMA's "Recommended Practices for Enhancing
  Digital Audio Compatibility in Multimedia Systems" (1992), and the WAV layouts of IMA and MS ADPCM by Microsoft's
  "New Multimedia Data Types and Data Techniques" (1994) (knowledge). All four decode with integer arithmetic, so
  each has one correct output (inferred). No patent pool is known for any of them (knowledge).
- **Documentation and WebView.** The public encoding table lists AAC, MP3, PCM, H.264 and H.265 only
  (`modules/javafx.media/src/main/java/javafx/scene/media/package-info.java:58-71`). WebView's `canPlayType` checks
  the container type against `MediaManager.getSupportedContentTypes()` and does not look at codecs
  (`modules/javafx.web/src/main/native/Source/WebCore/platform/graphics/java/MediaPlayerPrivateJava.cpp:225-228`), so
  its answers for `audio/x-wav` and `audio/x-aiff` stay as they are unless a type is added.

## Out of scope
- Other compressed encodings: OKI, IMA DK3 and DK4, G.722, G.726, GSM 6.10, MP3 inside WAV, and AIFC compression
  types other than A-law and µ-law, such as `ima4`. They keep failing early, as US-097 requires.
- A-law and µ-law in other containers, such as AU.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **They play.** On Windows, macOS and Linux, through `MediaPlayer` and `AudioClip`, these play to their end with
   the right duration, mono and stereo, at 8 kHz and 44.1 kHz, in every header form and chunk order US-097 accepts:
   - WAV with A-law or µ-law, by format tag and as an EXTENSIBLE sub-format;
   - WAV with IMA ADPCM (tag 0x0011) or MS ADPCM (tag 0x0002);
   - AIFC with the compression types `alaw`, `ALAW`, `ulaw` or `ULAW` (knowledge: the lower-case and upper-case
     spellings both occur).
2. **Exact samples.** Decoding gives 16-bit signed PCM, and every sample equals the value the specification
   defines, with no rounding added: G.711's tables for each of the 256 codes of each law, and the IMA and Microsoft
   ADPCM algorithms for every sample of the corpus.
3. **The corpus** covers every G.711 code; ADPCM in mono and stereo with several block sizes, a short last block, and
   both ends of the IMA step table and of the MS ADPCM adaptation table; and MS ADPCM files that use the seven
   standard coefficient pairs and one whose header carries a set of its own (knowledge: the header holds the
   coefficients).
4. **Broken input.** A truncated file or a header with impossible values (no channels, a block too small for its
   header) fails with a `MediaException` naming the fault, or plays what it holds and ends. It never hangs or crashes,
   and plays no sample the file does not hold.
5. **Same on every target.** The decoded samples are identical on the five CI targets.
6. **Documented, and WebView.** The public format tables list A-law, µ-law, IMA ADPCM and MS ADPCM, and the
   containers that carry each. Each content type added to `MediaManager.getSupportedContentTypes()` is listed here,
   and a test checks WebView's `canPlayType` for it.
7. **Nothing else broken.** The media module's tests pass with the counts of a run before the change, plus the new
   tests. All five CI legs are green.
